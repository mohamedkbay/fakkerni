# فكّرني

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

People capturing many short work reminders, often for the current week, primarily speaking Libyan Arabic.

## Product Purpose

Speak or type a task and event time, review a proposed reminder, approve it, and receive a local notification one hour before the event.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Android is native Java. The iOS companion is native SwiftUI. Arabic and English interfaces use Western digits. AI is optional; one connected provider is active at a time. Contacts are searched and matched on-device. A person approves every AI draft. API keys never ship in source or release binaries.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing فكّرني name, Cairo font, silver animated assistant, monochrome light/dark appearance, and Morphicons assets. The user explicitly rejected emoji and bright yellow UI. iOS uses native navigation and permission flows within this identity.

## Evidence on Hand

Android source, archived Android screenshots in docs/media, the existing vector mark, and the nine owner-supplied tones. New iOS screenshots must come from an iOS Simulator or device.

## Product Principles

- Capture first; keep settings short.
- Never schedule unapproved AI output or invent a contact's number.
- Keep manual entry and scheduled alerts independent of AI services.
- Show missing permissions and scheduling failures explicitly.
